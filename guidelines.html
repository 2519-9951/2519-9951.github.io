Author guidelines

Submit your manuscript as a Word document (.docx) to the Managing Editor. The manuscript checklist specifies a length of 3,000 to 6,000 words.

Submission and contact

Authorship

Apply all four ICMJE authorship criteria. Each author should:

  1. Contribute substantially to the conception or design of the study, or to collecting, analysing or interpreting its data.
  2. Draft the manuscript or critically revise its intellectual content.
  3. Approve the final version for publication.
  4. Accept accountability for the work and take responsibility for resolving questions about its accuracy or integrity.

Acknowledge other contributors and obtain their agreement, since acknowledgement can imply endorsement of the work.

Citations and references

Use APA author–date citations. Examples include (Ahmed, 2020), (Ahmed & Yamin, 2020) and (Ahmed et al., 2020). Arrange the unnumbered reference list alphabetically by first author, then chronologically for works by the same author or authors. Verify names, years, titles and publication details against the original sources.

Before submission

Confirm originality, include relevant funding and conflict-of-interest statements and check that citations match the reference list. Authors, reviewers and editors are asked to follow the journal’s ethical standards and COPE Core Practices.
