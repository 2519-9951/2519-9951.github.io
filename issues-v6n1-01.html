A Quantitative Analysis of the Distribution and Reserve of Heavy Minerals in Bangladesh's Sand-Covered Coastal Regions

Md. Tariqul Islam Bhuiyan, Tanjina Akter

Volume 6, Issue 1 · 2023 · pp. 1–4

Read full text on HAL

Abstract

Heavy minerals are a subset of minerals with a higher density than typical minerals. They have a variety of uses in industrial, scientific, and commercial applications. They play a crucial role in developing many modern technologies and scientific discoveries. Heavy minerals have various applications, including industrial minerals, gemstones, geological indicators, environmental monitoring, medical applications, and nuclear energy. The primary objectives of this research include the exploration & exploitation of heavy minerals and determining & monitoring of environmental radioactivity determination of the presence & impact of radioactive & trace elements in sand/soil, air, water, and marine biota along the coastal areas and offshore islands of Bangladesh. In this research, a sand sample was collected from several coastal areas of Bangladesh, and heavy minerals were extracted from the sample and identified on a laboratory scale. This study reported quantitative data on the reserve of sand and heavy minerals. This experiment also produced a reliable distribution of the heavy minerals in the sand. A big abundance of these minerals was found in the sand of coastal areas in Bangladesh. In contrast, the study of heavy minerals gives very effective information that can be used in commercial production. The commercial production of these minerals will significantly impact Bangladesh's economy. So, there is a lot of scope in this field from the perspective of Bangladesh. The entire study for this research was based on this motivation.
