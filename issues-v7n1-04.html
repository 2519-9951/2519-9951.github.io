Total Rewards as a Driver of Employee Performance: An HRM-Based Analytical Literature Review

Mina Manjurul Hossain Reza, Taposh Kumar Neogy

Volume 7, Issue 1 · 2024 · pp. 27–36

Read full text on HAL

Abstract

An effective Total Rewards System is a vital HRM strategy for attracting, retaining, and motivating employees to enhance organizational performance. This study conducts an extensive literature review to examine the relationship between total rewards, motivation, and employee performance. A thorough analysis of multiple research studies reveals a strong positive correlation between these components, indicating that well-structured total rewards significantly enhance employee motivation and, in turn, boost performance levels. Increased motivation leads to higher productivity, innovation, and overall job satisfaction, ultimately improving organizational success. The findings highlight that organizations implementing comprehensive reward systems experience lower turnover rates, improved workforce engagement, and increased profitability. However, the study is limited to analyzing employees' perceptions of total rewards and motivation within business organizations, without considering broader industry variations. This review consolidates existing research to provide valuable insights into HRM practices, helping organizations optimize reward structures to enhance employee commitment and performance. By integrating strategic reward mechanisms, HR professionals can create a work environment that fosters motivation and continuous growth, ensuring long-term organizational success. The study reinforces the need for clear, practical, and adaptable compensation models that align employee incentives with business objectives, making HRM-driven reward systems a critical factor in workforce efficiency.
